can only reject a sample, never certify one.

## Deviations from the pre-registration

All of these could have been seen before the run. They are recorded here
rather than silently worked around.

1. **`order_in_run` cannot be a regressor within a measurement.** It is
   constant for each measurement (`sd64_explicit` is always 1, and so on).
   Position in the process can only be compared *across* measurements, and
   there it is fully confounded with the config: the bench runs its 4
   configs in a fixed order (8 measurements, 6 parsed).
2. **Condition and `pre_pstate` are the same variable in this run.** All 15
   A samples entered at P0 (1770–1785 MHz) and all 15 B samples at P8
   (210 MHz). A regression with both terms is not estimable.
3. **The pre-registered regression was replaced** by Welch tests on means,
   per measurement. That is the estimable part of item 2 of the plan.
4. **`post_load_1m` was not analysed** in the first draft, although the
   pre-registration names it. It is now (Part 4c).
5. **The pre-registration said the SD64-specific hypothesis "is dead and is
   not tested".** Yet this write-up's headline is the SD64 spread. That
   spread is reported because `sd64_implicit` is the gate's config. It is
   post hoc emphasis, not a pre-registered test.

## Design limitation: what the A/B contrast did and did not reach

Found by review, after the run.

**Clock and P-state almost certainly did not reach the timed window.** The
bench runs `cpu_conv2d_nhwc_ref` before **every** config's GPU work
(`bench_implicit_gemm.cu:212-216` at `50bc147`; explicit timed at `:276-290`,
implicit at `:292-302`, same commit). It is several seconds of CPU per config. The reference is
skipped only for inputs over 16 MB, and all four configs are under that:
SD64 1.3 M floats, SD32 0.66 M, SD128 2.6 M, Small 0.26 M, against a 4.2 M
limit. A process takes about 19 s (median 19.2 s between samples); its
timed GPU work is about 0.9 s. So the GPU idles, and its clock and P-state
reset, before each config, whatever state the process entered in. The
**explicit** section starts right after that idle. The **implicit**
section, which is the gate's measurement, follows the explicit timing plus
20 warmup launches.

**Temperature very likely reached it.** Condition A entered at 56.7 °C
(55–59) and B at 50.9 °C (50–52), a ~6 °C contrast (Part 4g). Temperature
in the window itself is not measured. A few seconds of CPU-reference idle
is unlikely to erase the contrast: by the end of the whole ~19 s process
it is still 1.0 °C (57.9 vs 56.9 °C). Neither the mean nor the spread of `sd64_implicit`
differed detectably:
- mean: +0.5%, 95% CI −3.9% to +4.8%;
- sd ratio A/B: 1.00, 95% CI [0.58, 1.73];
- Brown-Forsythe p = 0.99.

With n = 15 per arm, a 1.5× difference in spread would go undetected.

**Condition B is not the gate's conv2d condition.** `bench_regress.R` has no
sleep. Gate rows are written 19–22 s apart *[gate log]*, against a ~19 s
conv2d process, so conv2d starts within a few seconds of `flash_attn`
ending. Condition A is the closer analogue. Neither
condition reproduces the gate's thermal context, which comes straight
after a power-bound kernel (see Gate history).

(Exploratory: the 10% rule is post hoc.)

Five of the six are in the **explicit** kernels. In each config, the
explicit kernel is timed first after the CPU reference has left the GPU
idle. The candidate mechanism is the clock ramp after that idle: a
hypothesis, recorded with the pilot and not measured here. Between the
idle and the timing there are also three correctness launches and
device-to-host copies.

The gate's 5935 reading (26% below the extract's maximum) is an
*implicit* reading. This campaign's deepest `sd64_implicit` value is 6788
(sample 20, −10.3%, in the table).

**A low episode in condition A** (Part 4j, post hoc):
- The four lowest A values of `sd64_implicit` are samples 7, 9, 11 and 13,
  consecutive in A order (P ≈ 0.009 under exchangeability).
- Samples 11 and 13 also hold two of the three `sd32_explicit` drops.
- That is a roughly 7-minute stretch in which two different kernels ran
  low together.
- Host load was **low** during it (1.29–1.76), so it is not load
  contamination.

## What this run shows, and what it cannot

The gate measures `conv2d_implicit_gemm` as `sd64_implicit`: the same
executable, section `SD 64`, match `Implicit (single kern)` (see
`data/baselines.json`).

1. **The A/B contrast found no effect, and could only reach part of the
   state.**
   - Entry clock/P-state almost certainly did not reach the timed window,
     because the CPU-reference idle resets it.
   - A ~6 °C entry-temperature contrast very likely reached it (not
     measured in the window; still 1.0 °C at process end).
   - Neither the mean (+0.5%, CI −3.9% to +4.8%) nor the spread (sd ratio
     1.00, CI 0.58–1.73) of `sd64_implicit` differed detectably.
   - A 1.5× difference in spread would not have been detected.
   - This does not test the gate's cadence: neither condition comes
     straight after a power-bound kernel.
2. **`sd64_implicit` spreads at a fixed 150 W regime:** rel sd 5.7%,
   range 6788–8107 (1.19×). Its implicit siblings stay within 0.86%
   (`sd32`) and 0.94% (`sd128`).
3. **The one push-blocking reading (5935, the 2026-07-31 REGRESSION) was
   not sampled.**
   - At the gate's own floor (6435) the campaign is consistent with the
     gate history: 1 of 28 vs 0 of 30, Fisher p = 0.48.
   - The gate's 6577 and 6751 are deep but **passing** (verdict OK).
   - At a 6800 cut: gate 3 of 28, campaign 1 of 30, p = 0.34 (Part 4h).
   - Explicit-kernel drops of the same depth *were* sampled (table above).
4. **Whether the spread comes from SD64's shape or SD64's position cannot
   be told from this design.** SD64 is always the first config in the
   process (deviation 1).

All 30 samples clear the gate's 10% floor below the 7150 baseline (6435).
The baseline is a median of 5 from 2026-05-10 and sits about 5% below
today's mean of 7531. For why re-baselining it needs care, see #219.

## Exploratory (NOT pre-registered)

**Bimodality: consistent with a single mode** (Part 4b). The largest gap in
the sorted `sd64_implicit` values is 166 GFLOPS (7611 to 7777). Under a
single normal distribution with the same n, mean and sd, a gap at least
that large occurs in 92% of draws. The observed max-gap/mean-spacing ratio
is 3.65, against a null median of 4.88. The first draft's "two clusters"
reading is withdrawn.

**Covariates, high vs low split at 7694** (Part 4a; uncorrected p,
about 12 variables examined):

| variable | high − low | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pre_load_1m` | +0.68 | [+0.17, +1.18] | 0.011 |
| `post_load_1m` | +0.42 | [−0.16, +1.00] | 0.15 |
| `sd32_explicit` | +256 | [−14, +526] | 0.061 |
| `sd64_explicit` | +157 | [−72, +387] | 0.17 |
| `sd128_explicit` | −40 | [−121, +40] | 0.30 |
| `sd128_implicit` | +25 | [−24, +74] | 0.31 |
| `sd32_implicit` | +8 | [−23, +38] | 0.61 |

Condition (A 8 high / 7 low, B 6 / 9), `post_clock_sm` (1760 vs 1759),
`post_temp_c` (57.4 vs 57.4) and `post_power_w` (66.7 vs 67.5) do not
differ.

**For `sd64_implicit`, host load is the one variable nominally associated
with the high samples.** It does not survive a correction for about 12
comparisons. Its direction, more load with *higher* throughput, is the
opposite of contamination. It is partly carried by the A low episode
above: without samples 7–13, r in A falls from +0.53 to +0.36 (p = 0.27).
The threshold-free statistic is r = +0.37 over all 30 (p = 0.043).

For a different measurement, `sd32_implicit` in A, the load signal runs in
the contamination direction (see Host load). "No recorded variable
separates the groups", the first draft, and "host load is anti-
contamination", the first correction, were both too broad.

The cause is not located. Per-process state, a clock ramp after the
CPU-reference idle, host contamination in some cells, and an episodic
state like the A low episode all remain candidates. No comparison between
them has been made.

## Gate history (exploratory; `gate_conv2d_history.tsv`, Parts 4e, 4h, 4i, 4l)

The gate runs conv2d last. It starts within a few seconds of `flash_attn`
ending, with no sleep, and after `igemm_pipelined_cpasync`, which hits
`SwPowerCap` at 125–137 W *[gate log]* and 57–75 °C (extract). This
campaign ran conv2d alone, on a GPU at 50–62 °C. **The gate's thermal
context, straight after a power-bound kernel, was not reproduced.** The
first correction dropped this caveat; it is restored.

The extract holds 28 non-throttled readings of this config (2026-07-31,
2026-08-12 and 2026-11-02, including five from today's later pushes):

`5935 6577 6751 6908 7043 7052 7104 7205 7215 7543 7612 7648 7733 7748
7751 7813 7905 7967 8006 8028 8031 8034 8038 8067 8068 8070 8071 8081`

- **Shape: a ceiling near 8030 plus a spread tail**, as the 2026-08-13
  #195 analysis said. The first draft's "same two levels" left out the
  readings in between and is withdrawn.
- **The ceiling's tightness matches the campaign's.** At the same 7900
  cut, the gate's 12 readings have sd 51 and the campaign's 8 have sd 49,
  so there is no difference in shape (Part 4i). The first correction
  compared sds at different cut-offs.
- **The ceiling was absent today.** The 8 gate readings of 2026-11-02 peak
  at 7905 and average 7340. All 10 readings at or above 8000 are from July
  and August.
- **Today the gate ran lower than the campaign** (post hoc, Part 4l). The
  gate's 8 readings put 2 below 6788 (6577, 6751), against 0 of 30 in the
  campaign (Fisher p = 0.040). Mann-Whitney p = 0.25, means 7340 vs 7531.
  This mildly favours "the gate's cadence contributes".
- **#195's 1.36× is max/min of this extract**, 8081/5935.
  - 5935 is the 2026-07-31 15:44 REGRESSION, the push-blocking event #195
    is about, and it is not an outlier to set aside.
  - Without it the span is 1.23×.
  - 6577 and 6751 are deep but passed (verdict OK).
- **The power-capped igemm is not sufficient for a low reading; whether it
  is necessary cannot be tested from this record.**
  - Of the 24 runs where igemm hit `SwPowerCap`, conv2d read high in 13 and
    low in 11.
  - Only 3 runs had no igemm (2 high, 1 low), and they are no clean
    control: they started 80–129 s after a capped run's start *[gate log]*.
  - All three readings below 6788 followed a capped igemm.
  - The first draft's "ruled out by the trace" and the first correction's
    "not necessary" are both withdrawn.

## Next steps (proposed, not run; see #223)

1. **Time each launch inside the bench.** It currently times 100 launches
   with one `BenchTimer` (CUevent pair) and reports the mean. Record an
   event pair per launch, and keep the distribution. That would show
   whether a low reading is a few slow launches (a ramp, a throttle event)
   or a uniformly slow window.
2. **Remove the idle before timing.** Spin the GPU, or compute the CPU
   reference outside the config loop, so that the timed section starts
   from a known clock state. If the explicit-kernel drops and the
   `sd64_implicit` spread shrink, the clock ramp is the mechanism.
3. **Permute the config order** to separate SD64's shape from its
   position. The first correction dropped this step on a false premise:
   it claimed the CPU-reference interval depends on the shape.
   - The reference does identical work for SD64, SD32 and SD128:
     3,774,873,600 MACs each (Part 4k).
   - Its weight arrays differ: 3.7, 14.7 and 0.9 MB. So wall time could
     differ through cache effects, but that is unmeasured.
   - The step is reinstated.
4. **Reproduce the gate's sequence.** Run conv2d straight after a
   power-bound kernel, which neither condition here did.
5. **Telemetry last.** A log only helps if it resolves the window: 100 ms
   gives about one sample per ~106 ms window. It must run at a fixed rate
   for the whole session, so every condition is loaded equally.

The warmups before each timed section are 20 launches, 20–42 ms depending
on the measurement (SD64: 20–24 ms). Whether that is short against the
clock ramp is unmeasured.
